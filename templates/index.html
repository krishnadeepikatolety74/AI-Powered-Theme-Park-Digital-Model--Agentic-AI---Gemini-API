About ParkOps

The Digital Model Engine for Next-Gen Theme Parks

ParkOps creates a live 1:1 synchronized simulation of physical attractions, queuing zones, pedestrian paths, and guest services.

⚡ Real-Time IoT Ingestion

Connects turnstile sensors, ride dispatch PLCs, GPS wristbands, and concession point-of-sale systems into a unified low-latency telemetry bus.

🧠 Single AI Agent Pipeline

Continuously executes the 4-stage operational intelligence cycle: MONITOR live feeds, PREDICT crowd bottlenecks, SIMULATE virtual interventions, and RECOMMEND staff allocations.

🎯 Dynamic Guest Happiness

Reduces average wait times by up to 28% through synchronized mobile app push incentives, virtual queue slot balancing, and early surge warnings.
